Why did I lose?!

◂ Home

Drop in the combat log you exported from the game (one or several). You'll get a plain answer for each fight, and if you add wins and losses against the same enemy, what changed between them.

Or drag the files here. They're read in this browser only and aren't uploaded or saved.

The answer is worked out from the damage numbers in the log: how much of your damage their Apex Barrier, mitigation and Isolytic Defense absorbed, how much of theirs got through to you, and how close the fight was. It's a guide, not a combat simulator. Fan-made; not affiliated with Scopely or Paramount.
